About this guide

Leonida Ledger is an offline, static guide: it loads no network resources and works with the network off. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them (one name is censored with asterisks by PlayStation itself: “T**s Ass Drug Money”). Every guide text is written in our own words for this entry and names its sources beside it — a fact with no source earns no trust here. Where a method is still unconfirmed (Bad Luck, I Guess; The Truth Is Out There; Challenging Tank), the guide says so instead of inventing one.

Online trophies: none

0 online trophies. All 55 trophies are earned in single-player, offline play. There is no multiplayer trophy, no co-op trophy and no trophy that needs PlayStation Plus. If a filter for “online” shows an empty list, that is the correct answer — this box is the empty state, not a bug. Cheats disable trophies while active, so keep them off for a trophy run.

Sources for the trophy list and the guides: PowerPyx, Push Square, PlanetGTA6 (cross-checked against Exophase and PSNProfiles), KeenGamer, GTABase and EGamers. Lists were checked against at least two of these before the guide was written.
